How a score becomes metres

local depth = Hmax(storm) × score

The maps are unitless 0–1. Hmax is the engine’s wet-cell ceiling for that storm — not a gauge on this road. Score 0 stays dry. JRC then turns metres into a damage ratio for this construction class. Land and frame survive, so the curve never reaches 100%.
